Android app · course project

Semester

Builds a clash-free course timetable from your university's schedule, keeps you focused during classes and study time, and plans each day around them. Everything runs on the phone: no AI, no account, no internet permission.

Try it in the browser

▶ Launch Semester in your browser

This is the real release APK streamed from an Android device in the cloud (Appetize.io). The button opens it on appetize.io in a new tab: press Tap to Start, wait a few seconds for the phone to boot, and use the mouse as your finger. Free sessions are short and may wait in a queue; reload to start again.

  1. Timetable → Courses: tap Try sample courses. The demo schedule opens in the review screen; nothing is saved until you tap Confirm and add courses.
  2. Tap each course's Choose chip: mark CS201 and MA202 Must take, the rest Want (ranked).
  3. Choices shows the ranking (Move up / down). Week shows every section, overlaps included.
  4. Results shows the top 3 clash-free timetables in milliseconds. Open Limits and preferences to set a credit cap, free days or no early classes and watch the answer change; lock a section to keep it in every option.
  5. Focus (Session / Apps / Insights) and Planner (Day / Tasks / Review): add a task, tap Auto-fill to place it in a free gap.

App blocking, Do Not Disturb, alarms and calendar writing need permissions that a cloud device can't fully grant; they were tested on a real Galaxy M15 (Android 15).

What it does

Timetable builder

Import a course-schedule PDF, CSV or pasted table; check it in a review screen; mark MUST and ranked WANT courses. A branch-and-bound scheduler returns the top 3 clash-free timetables, or names the smallest set of MUST courses that can't fit together. One tap writes the classes to the phone calendar.

Focus

App blocker with Pomodoro sessions and strict mode, automatic focus during every class, Do Not Disturb, YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels blocking, daily app limits, usage insights and streaks. Phone, SMS, emergency, maps, clock and Settings are never blocked.

Daily planner

A nightly prompt fills tomorrow's free gaps with your tasks; confirm and the plan goes to the calendar with reminders, focus sessions and a wake-up alarm. Review, carry-over, weekday templates, attendance, deadlines and a weekly summary.

How it was built

StackKotlin 2.4, Jetpack Compose (Material 3), Room, multi-module Gradle (pure-Kotlin core: model, parser, scheduler, planner)
ProcessSpec-driven: every requirement has an ID and an acceptance test (e.g. A07_…); decisions and findings logged as D-xx / I-xx
Tests164 JVM + 52 instrumented tests (emulator, API 34); guided checks on a Galaxy M15 with Android 15
NumbersScheduler 10 courses × 5 options: 6 ms on the phone · app blocked within ~200 ms · 30-page PDF read in ~3 s · APK 8 MB
GuaranteesNo INTERNET permission (checked on every build) · parsed data used only after review · the app touches only its own calendar events and alarms
Made withClaude Code, over six sessions (22–26 Oct 2026). The full turn-by-turn conversation is in the build transcript.

Install on a phone

Download semester.apk on an Android 8.0+ phone, open it and allow "install unknown apps" for your browser. Or with a phone connected over USB: adb install semester.apk.
